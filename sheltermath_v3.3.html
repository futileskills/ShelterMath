// SHELTERMATH v3.3.beta

What this tool does: Estimates how much a building structure reduces indoor gamma radiation dose from nuclear fallout, expressed as a Protection Factor (PF). Uses FEMA REP-10 methodology with physically-correct multiplicative layer attenuation, Lambertian view factor geometry, NCRP-165 ground-shine corrections, and per-floor overhead corrections.

Limitations: This is a personal educational tool, not certified engineering software. Outputs approximate real-world performance. Factors not modeled include irregular geometry, penetrations, HVAC pathways, scattered radiation streaming, and material non-uniformity. Use NukeMap or official FEMA data for dose-rate inputs.

Official resources: FEMA, CDC, and your local emergency management agency. This tool does not replace professional engineering assessment.

Privacy: All calculations run entirely in your browser. No data is ever transmitted to any server.

Dose Pathways Modeled
▸ Ground-shine — dominant path. Gamma from fallout deposited on soil, attenuated by wall mass. Accounts for ~50% of dose on ground floor (geometry-weighted).
▸ Skyshine ☁ — scattered gamma arriving from sky hemisphere (not ground). Adds ~15–40% of open-field dose through the roof, even through thick material. Modeled per NCRP-165 as a fixed fraction (f_sky) × roof transmission. Ground floor only.
▸ Neighbor shielding — adjacent rooms on the same floor add extra wall mass between you and the source. Detected automatically; shown in teal on room edges.
▸ Floor/slab attenuation — the floor slab partially shields upwelling ground-shine. Modeled as ~50% geometric reduction for ground floor.
▸ Basement soil shielding — below-grade soil attenuates gamma exponentially. Deeper basements in denser soil dramatically raise PF.
Floor Level Effects
BSMTBest protection. Soil shielding on all sides. Depth multiplies PF.
GNDModerate. Skyshine and direct ground-shine both apply. Wall mass critical.
2NDFloor path reduced to 10% (no ground contact). Overhead path still exposed.
3RD+Floor path 3%. Roof exposure significant. Upper floors perform poorly.
PF = 1/dose_fraction. A PF of 10 means 1/10th the outdoor dose rate. Not all pathways are modeled (HVAC, streaming, scatter through openings). Values are approximations — use for relative comparison and planning.
